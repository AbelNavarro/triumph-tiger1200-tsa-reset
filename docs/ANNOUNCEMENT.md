# Announcement draft

**Solved: Triumph Tiger 1200 TSA quickshifter fault after sensor repair**

Three weeks ago the quickshifter on my 2018 Triumph Tiger 1200 XRT stopped working because of damaged shift-force sensor wiring. I repaired it, but the warning remained. Clearing fault codes P1702 and P1703 was not enough.

After investigating the ECU diagnostic behaviour and adaptation process, I finally restored the quickshifter on November 26, 2026. The ECU accepted the adaptation reset, and after the remaining gear learning and a short ride the dashboard warning disappeared.

I've published the original diagnostic and guided reset software under the MIT license, along with research findings and offline tests. The original workflow worked on my own motorcycle; the public derivative has not been separately validated on hardware. Other models are unverified. This is independent experimental repair software, not an official Triumph tool.

https://github.com/AbelNavarro/triumph-tiger1200-tsa-reset

I hope this saves someone else three weeks of their life.
